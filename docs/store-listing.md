# Play Store listing (draft)

**App name (30):** Zone 2 Running

**Short description (80):** Get faster at an easy heart rate. A plan, a coach and your progress, all on your phone.

**Full description**

Most runners run too hard, too often. Zone 2 Running helps you slow down on purpose, and shows you getting faster at the same easy heart rate.

Tell it a recent pace and heart rate. It works out your heart-rate zones, your Zone 2 pace today, and a week-by-week plan toward the pace you want.

What you get
• A plan built from your pace, your heart rate and the days you can run
• Guided live runs with GPS, spoken cues and an alert when you leave Zone 2 (with a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor)
• Automatic import from Health Connect, so runs from your watch app just appear
• Your Zone 2 pace over time, with a plain note on how solid the estimate is
• A home screen that changes through the day: the session in the morning, the result after your run
• Nine two-minute lessons on easy running
• Weekly streaks that forgive a missed week, quests, levels and badges, all optional
• Race goal with taper, kilometre splits, heat adjustment, miles or kilometres

Private by design
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• Your runs stay on your phone
• Goes online only if you switch on weather or update checks
• Open source under the MIT licence

Not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training plan if you have a health condition.

## Data safety form

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app collect or share user data? | No data is collected by the developer. |
| Location | Used on the device during a live run; not collected, not shared. |
| Health and fitness | Read from Health Connect and stored on the device; not collected, not shared. |
| Is data encrypted in transit? | The only network requests (weather, update check) use HTTPS. |
| Can users request deletion? | Uninstalling removes everything; there is nothing held elsewhere. |

Health Connect apps also need Google's Health Connect declaration form, stating that exercise, heart rate and distance are read to show training progress.
